3. Specific VanSwap Risks

💸 Impermanent Loss HIGH RISK

Impermanent loss is one of the most significant risks for liquidity providers.

When you provide liquidity to a pool, you may experience impermanent loss if the price ratio of the tokens changes compared to when you deposited them. This loss occurs because of the automated market maker formula and can result in receiving back less valuable assets than if you had simply held the tokens.

  • Higher volatility = higher impermanent loss
  • Loss can exceed earned trading fees
  • Permanent if tokens are withdrawn during price divergence
  • Affects all liquidity providers in AMM protocols
🔄 Slippage and Price Impact MEDIUM RISK

Large trades can significantly move market prices on decentralized exchanges.

  • Higher slippage on low-liquidity pools
  • Price impact percentage shown before trading
  • Front-running and MEV (Miner Extractable Value)
  • Transaction ordering dependencies
